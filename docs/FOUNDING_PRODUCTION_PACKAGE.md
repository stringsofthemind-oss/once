# Founding production package — proposal for validation

**Internal commercial experiment; not an available production service or an
approved price list.** Repository hosted-readiness gates remain in force. No
live billing, release or deployment is authorized by this document.

## Smallest product worth paying for

One supported consequential workflow with managed durable coordination, a
maintained provider recovery adapter and an owner for unresolved outcomes.
Do not select the first workflow because an adapter already exists.
Stripe refunds are an integration candidate, but native idempotency is a
strong substitute. Qualify for recurring recovery/operational work, not merely
a missing idempotency header. Research agent-created orders, bookings and
account mutations where native protections or recovery are inadequate.
GitHub issue creation is a safe research effect, not a validated paid wedge.
No initial commercial workflow has yet been selected by cold-user evidence.

Hypothesis: **£200/month excluding VAT**, one production project plus staging,
one agreed workflow, two users, inspectable operation history, UNKNOWN alerts,
bounded recovery assistance and onboarding. These contents are acceptance
requirements to validate; not all are implemented or live. Choose operation
allowance, retention and support limits from measured costs before selling.

The local SDK remains useful and free. Charge for operating safety, not for
the ability to block an unsafe retry. Meter unique admitted logical operations;
do not bill each replay, reconciliation or retry. Existing recovery must remain
accessible when new-effect admission is blocked by quotas or subscriptions.
Resolve orphaned meter reservations in the billing policy before live charging.

## Three design partners first

Target small B2B teams deploying support/commerce agents within 60 days, with
consequential writes, retrying workers and a named engineering owner. Trigger:
production launch, a real retry incident or repeated manual recovery work.
Start through one framework/tool integration and a provider sandbox proof.

Pilot acceptance:

1. Trusted code persists intent before dispatch and binds the actual effect.
2. Actual runtime/tool invocation goes through Once; no equivalent raw tool is exposed.
3. First execution/replay/conflict and lost-ack recovery pass in the provider sandbox.
4. Provider effects are independently counted; unresolved truth remains blocked.
5. State continuity, deployed service version and adapter assumptions are reviewed.
6. Operator ownership, support scope and recurring payment acceptance are agreed.

Production delivery still requires explicit owner approval of the actual
deployment/live-provider change. A local fixture or sandbox checkout is not MRR.

## Measurable acquisition funnel

Track organizations, qualified conversations, pilots started, independently
verified protected actions, paid recurring accounts and 30/60/90-day retention.
Measure UNKNOWN age/resolution, onboarding time and support hours. No opt-in
telemetry is introduced by this document.

Working target: approach 20 relevant organizations/week, hold about three
qualified conversations, and start at most one pilot/week while keeping at most
two new integrations active. Founder outreach is an activity plan, not an
assumed conversion rate. No messages are sent by this implementation.

Revenue alternatives:

| Model | Accounts for £2,000 MRR | Early tradeoff |
|---|---:|---|
| £200/month | 10 | Requires real operated value; support must be bounded |
| £100/month | 20 | More acquisition and support; useful if teams self-onboard |
| £50/month | 40 | High account burden before onboarding is repeatable |

Use £200 as the first paid-pilot hypothesis. Seek three recurring commitments
before adding tiers. If at least 15 qualified conversations and five completed
pilots produce fewer than two paying customers, revise the workflow/segment
before adding integrations or discounting. Conversion, retention and time to
£2,000 MRR remain unknown; calculate a forecast only after collecting cohorts.

Retention reason: production execution and uncertain-outcome recovery are
operationally useful every month. Expansion follows additional proven workflows
or volume, not extra agents. If Stripe alone is enough for the cohort, choose
one repeated non-native provider need rather than claiming broader safety.
